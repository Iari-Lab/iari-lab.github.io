Who is responsible

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the founding team of IARI Labs, named with its address in our Impressum. You can reach us at info@iari.io for anything about your data.

Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser sends GitHub the technical data every web request carries: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser and operating system. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in delivering the website securely and reliably. GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so transfers to the USA rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement.

The fonts, images and videos at the top of the page are served from this website itself. Opening a page makes no connection to Google or any other third party.

Visitor statistics (Google Analytics), only with your consent

If you press “Allow” in the banner, we use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, to learn how the site is used: the pages viewed and for how long, the type of device and browser, the approximate region, how you reached us, and whether you played a video or used the contact form. Google Analytics stores cookies on your device (_ga and _ga_<ID>, for up to two years) and processes the data on Google's servers, which may include Google LLC in the USA (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Art. 45 GDPR). According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We keep the event data for 2 months.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. Without it, the Google Analytics script is not loaded at all. You can withdraw your consent at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page; declining deletes the Google Analytics cookies on our domain and stops any further collection. See Google's privacy policy.

Videos from YouTube

Our videos are hosted on YouTube, a service of Google Ireland Limited. Until you press play, the page shows only a still image stored on our own server and nothing is loaded from YouTube. When you press play, the video is loaded from youtube-nocookie.com: Google then receives your IP address and the page you are on, and YouTube may store cookies or similar data on your device and process data in the USA (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

Legal basis: your consent, given by pressing play after the notice below the videos, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. See Google's privacy policy.

E-mail and the contact form

The contact form sends nothing by itself: it opens your own e-mail program with the message prepared, and you decide whether to send it. When you write to info@iari.io, we process your message and contact details to answer you and, where it applies, to prepare a pilot or another cooperation.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps before a contract) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering enquiries). Our e-mail is hosted by Google Workspace (Google Ireland Limited) as our processor under a data processing agreement; Google may process data in the USA (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). We delete your messages when they are no longer needed, unless a statutory retention period applies.

LinkedIn

The footer links to our LinkedIn page. It is a plain link: nothing is loaded from LinkedIn until you click it, and LinkedIn's own privacy policy applies there.

Your rights

  • Withdrawal of a consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).
  • A complaint to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one responsible for us: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, datenschutz-berlin.de.

Write to info@iari.io to use these rights. We make no automated decisions and do no profiling. You do not have to give us any personal data, but without your IP address the site cannot be delivered to you.
